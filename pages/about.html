Chapter 01 Founded with a simple idea

Clothes should understand childhood.

Maison Miette began with a feeling familiar to many parents: that children’s clothing too often asked little ones to choose between looking beautifully dressed and feeling entirely themselves.

We wanted to make pieces that could hold both. Clothes with the quiet refinement parents reach for on meaningful days, and the softness, room and resilience children need for everything those days become.

That meant beginning with the realities of family life—not an idealised wardrobe. Waistbands needed to feel easy after lunch. Sleeves had to layer without bunching. Seams should sit gently, fastenings should make sense, and every silhouette needed to allow for sitting cross-legged, climbing one more step and running without hesitation.

We design for the photograph—and for every wonderfully unplanned moment just outside its frame.

Parents taught us to value what earns its place in a drawer: colours that pair without thought, fabrics that grow softer with washing, and details that feel special without becoming difficult to care for. Children taught us the rest. They showed us where a garment pulls, what gets in the way and how quickly good design disappears into confident movement.

So each Maison Miette piece is made to be worn often and passed along with a story already inside it. We believe the most meaningful clothes are not kept perfect. They are softened by ordinary mornings, marked by celebrations and remembered because a child was free to live fully in them.

Worn. Loved. Remembered.
